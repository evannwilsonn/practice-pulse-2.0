The ledger

On a phone the ledger shows one column: the tab or office you picked. Small figures are the change against the prior period.

Ratios are worked from the period’s summed totals, not averages of monthly ratios. Write-offs are deductions, shown in parentheses. Red marks losses and the auditor’s exceptions; a letter beside a figure points to the exceptions schedule below the trial balance.
