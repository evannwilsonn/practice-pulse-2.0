The ledger

On a phone the ledger shows one column at a time; pick an office above to change it. Small figures are the change against the prior period (for year to date, the same months a year earlier).

Every ratio is worked from the period’s summed totals, not an average of monthly ratios. Operating profit is collections less payroll, supplies, lab fees, overhead and ad spend: a management figure, not GAAP. Losses are shown in red, in parentheses.
